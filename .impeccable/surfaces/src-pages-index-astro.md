# Home page

Scope: `/`, the first thing every visitor sees. Mode: Experience.

Audience and job: anyone looking Mikey up. In one screen they should learn his name,
what he does and where, and what he is doing this season, and have one thing to press.

Constraints: one full-screen seasonal photograph on a black page; the name centered at
the top with the tagline under it; the name opens the menu; a "Now" card; the ASCII
loading screen stays. The owner will supply the photo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fashion house's home page, for a person: a logo, one photograph, a few small
words. It refuses the portfolio default of a headline, a paragraph, and a card grid,
and it refuses decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Black and white. A small upright gothic name. Twelve-pixel tracked caps and
thirteen-pixel Courier. Hairline rectangles. One full-bleed photograph with a faded
film treatment as the only colour and texture.

STORY: The visitor sees the photograph and the name, reads the trade and the season,
and presses the one button or opens the menu.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The photograph fills the screen. Header over its top: "Menu" left, the
name centered with the tagline under it, San Diego date and time right. Bottom left on
the container line: a flat black card with the season and what is being built, and
under it one hairline button.

FORM: Fashion campaign page (Chrome Hearts base; Supreme and Vivienne Westwood behind
it). seed: none (pinned)

MOTION: The photograph develops once on load; the card and button fade up. Nothing loops.
