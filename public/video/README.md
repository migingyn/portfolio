# Section videos

One muted loop per section, from your own footage, 5 to 10 seconds each.

| Section | Video | Poster (shown before the video loads and under reduced motion) |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `home.mp4` | `home.jpg` |
| Projects | `projects.mp4` | `projects.jpg` |
| Experience | `experience.mp4` | `experience.jpg` |
| Resume | `resume.mp4` | `resume.jpg` |
| Blog | `blog.mp4` | `blog.jpg` |
| Seasons | `seasons.mp4` | `seasons.jpg` |
| Links | `links.mp4` | `links.jpg` |

Drop the files in this folder and rebuild. A section with no file shows a placeholder.
A season can point Home at a different clip with `video:` in its season file.
