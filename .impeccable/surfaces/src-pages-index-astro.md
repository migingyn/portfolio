# Home page

Scope: `/`, the first thing every visitor sees. Mode: Experience.

Audience and job: a recruiter or hiring manager first, then founders and other
builders. In ten seconds they should learn his name, his trade and city, and have one
obvious next step (the projects), with the resume and GitHub beside it.

Constraints: DESIGN.md v1.0, written by the owner, pins the whole direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, dark, media-led page where the owner's footage and three signature
moments do the talking. It refuses the portfolio default of a headline, a paragraph,
and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black canvas, warm off-white text, one grey, hairlines, one season
accent in tiny places. One sans family. Square everything. A full-frame muted video
loop behind each section. An ASCII character and a circular dial.

STORY: A small character changes on a black screen, the page fades in, and the visitor
reads a name, a trade, and a place, then goes to the projects or opens the dial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen video (placeholder until footage arrives). Logo top left.
Season name top right. Along the left edge at mid height: the name large, the tagline,
one outlined button, two links. The bottom-left corner is empty.

FORM: Pinned by the owner's DESIGN.md v1.0 (references: kapital.jp, lafour.com,
anygivenmoment.co, anaiis.world). seed: none (pinned)

MOTION: Loading screen about two seconds, then a fade. The dial turns in 400ms.
Sections crossfade in 240ms. The ASCII character appears on the loading screen only.
