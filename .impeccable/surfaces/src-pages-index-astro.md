# Home page

Scope: `/`, the first thing every visitor sees. Mode: Experience.

Audience and job: anyone looking Mikey up. In one screen they should learn his name,
what he does, what he is doing now, and how to get to projects and the resume.

Constraints: one full-screen seasonal image on a dark canvas; the name centered at the
top with the tagline under it; the name opens the menu; small footer links; a "Now"
readout with live San Diego time. The owner will supply the photo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one frame from a Y2K digicam: a single grainy photograph
filling the screen, with the site's facts burned into it like a date stamp. It refuses
the portfolio default of a headline, a paragraph, and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground. One full-bleed photograph, desaturated, bloomed, and
vignetted, under moving film grain. Bone-white type. A slanted gothic name with soft
halation instead of a cartoon offset. An orange dot-matrix date stamp. Flat
rectangles for controls. No outlines, bursts, bubbles, or bright flat inks.

STORY: The visitor sees a mood and a name, reads what Mikey does and what he is doing
this season, and either opens the menu from the name or follows a section line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The photo covers the whole viewport. Name at top center, about 128px,
tagline under it. Bottom left: season line and the Now readout as small ad copy.
Bottom right: section lines, then the live date stamp. A small recording mark and
running timecode sit top left. The primary action is the name (opens the menu).

FORM: Digicam frame with burned-in overlay. seed: none (pinned)

MOTION: After the loading screen lifts, the photo develops from blown-out and soft to
exposed. Then only quiet loops: grain, timecode, clock.
